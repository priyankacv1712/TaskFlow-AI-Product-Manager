# TaskFlow Company Goals

Current business goals:

1. Increase new-user activation.
2. Improve 30-day user retention.
3. Reduce onboarding abandonment.
4. Increase adoption among small and medium businesses.
5. Improve team collaboration usage.
6. Maintain a simple and lightweight product experience.

Primary product success metrics:

- activation rate
- 7-day retention
- 30-day retention
- weekly active teams
- feature adoption
- onboarding completion rate
