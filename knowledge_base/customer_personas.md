# TaskFlow Customer Personas

## Persona 1: Small Business Owner

Needs:
- simple setup
- easy team collaboration
- clear project visibility
- minimal technical knowledge required

Pain Points:
- complex onboarding
- expensive enterprise tools
- too many configuration options

## Persona 2: Project Manager

Needs:
- task tracking
- reporting
- team visibility
- deadlines and milestones

Pain Points:
- reports that are difficult to find
- inconsistent team adoption
- slow analytics

## Persona 3: Team Member

Needs:
- quick task updates
- clear priorities
- easy invitations
- simple notifications

Pain Points:
- confusing navigation
- excessive setup
- too many unnecessary features
