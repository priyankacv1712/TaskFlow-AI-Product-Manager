# TaskFlow Product Policies

Product teams should follow these principles:

1. Major roadmap decisions require Product Manager approval.
2. Security-related changes require engineering and security review.
3. Accessibility should be considered for all major user-facing features.
4. Existing customers should not lose functionality during product changes.
5. Product decisions should be supported by customer, analytics, or market evidence.
6. AI-generated recommendations must remain subject to human review.
