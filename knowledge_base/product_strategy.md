# TaskFlow Product Strategy

TaskFlow is a SaaS project-management platform designed primarily for small and medium businesses.

The product strategy focuses on:

- fast onboarding
- simple project setup
- team collaboration
- lightweight reporting
- reliable performance
- ease of use for non-technical users

TaskFlow should avoid excessive feature complexity that makes the platform difficult for small teams to use.

The primary product positioning is:
"A fast, simple and practical project-management platform for growing teams."
